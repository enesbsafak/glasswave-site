Getting started

Open Glasswave and tap Add a Source. Choose Xtream Codes (server address, username and password), M3U (a playlist URL or a file) or Stalker (portal address and the MAC address your provider registered). Your channels, movies and series download once and then open instantly.

Frequently asked questions

Does Glasswave include channels?
No. Glasswave is a player only. Add a source you are entitled to use.

A stream says "The provider is busy".
Many subscriptions allow one stream at a time. Close the stream on your other devices and apps; Glasswave tries again for you.

The TV guide is empty.
Xtream sources use the panel's own guide. For M3U, add a guide (XMLTV) URL in Settings → your source. You can also fix a channel's guide match from its long-press menu.

How do I pause and rewind live TV?
Timeshift is on by default (Settings → Playback). Pause, rewind 10 seconds, and tap the LIVE badge to return.

Picture in Picture or AirPlay video?
Turn on Native player for HLS and choose HLS (m3u8) as the live stream format in Settings → Playback.

Can I move my setup to a new device?
Settings → Backup & Restore exports an encrypted file, protected by a password you choose.
